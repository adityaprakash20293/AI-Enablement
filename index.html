Using AI responsibly at work

A practical starting point for using AI to support work while keeping human judgement and accountability in place.

Start with a clear taskDescribe the outcome you need, the audience, and the format. Break complex requests into smaller steps.
Provide useful contextShare approved, non-sensitive background information and explain relevant constraints.
Review the outputCheck facts, calculations, tone, completeness, and alignment with current procedures.
Protect informationFollow organizational policies. Do not enter confidential, personal, or restricted information into unapproved tools.
Key principle: AI can help create a first draft or organize information. A knowledgeable person remains responsible for checking and approving the result.
